96643022 SOCIAL ENTREPRENEURS 3 (3-0-6)
PREREQUISITE : NONE
Study the role of social entrepreneur in selecting social problems of adding the value of local business by the principles of business both planning and managing the system in order to resolve those social problems without the emphasis on individual profit from the business.
