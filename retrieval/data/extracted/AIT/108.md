TEAM-PROJECT 1
รายวิชาบังคับก่อน : ไม่มี
PREREQUISITE : NONE

TEAM-PROJECT 2

Small teams of students undertake the design, implementation, testing, evaluation, and presentation of a specific project of size and complexity suitable for novice students.
